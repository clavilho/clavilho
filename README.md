My primary expertise is in **backend development with C# and .NET**, building APIs, business services, integrations, and maintainable application architectures. ## 🏛️ Engineering Principles

I believe great software engineering goes beyond making an endpoint work. It's about understanding the system as a whole and making thoughtful decisions that support long-term evolution.

| Principle | My approach |
|---|---|
| **Separation of concerns** | Keep business rules independent from infrastructure details whenever practical. |
| **Maintainability** | Favor readable code, clear boundaries, and explicit responsibilities. |
| **Scalability** | Consider asynchronous processing, workload distribution, and database performance. |
| **Reliability** | Investigate failure scenarios, integration errors, and operational behavior. |
| **Data integrity** | Consider transaction boundaries, duplicate messages, and consistency between services. |
| **Continuous improvement** | Refactor incrementally and improve existing systems while respecting business requirements. |
